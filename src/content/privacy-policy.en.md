## 2. Purposes and legal bases of processing

We process personal data for the following purposes and on the following legal bases under Art. 6 GDPR:

- **Providing and operating the Application** (e.g. account and app functionality via our backend infrastructure) — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).
- **Error and performance monitoring** (via Sentry) — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in ensuring a stable, functioning, and secure Application).
- **Responding to contact/feedback form inquiries** — Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering your enquiry).

## 3. Automatically collected data

When you use the Application, certain technical data may be automatically collected, including your IP address, device model, operating system and version, device identifiers, and session identifiers. This data is used exclusively to:

- identify and monitor software errors and crashes;
- measure API call performance and page/screen load times;
- debug issues and improve the stability and performance of the Application.

This data does not include personally identifiable information (PII) and is not used to individually identify you.

## 4. Contact / Feedback form

If you contact us via a feedback or contact form, we process the information you provide (such as your email address and message content) solely to answer your enquiry. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in responding to your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We retain this data only for as long as necessary to address your enquiry, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## 5. Advertising and recommendation data

The Application does not use advertising SDKs, ad networks, or behavioral tracking to serve personalized advertisements, and no data is collected or processed for advertising or ad-recommendation purposes.

## 6. Essential / functional tools (no consent required)

We use the Android and iOS Sentry SDK to identify and monitor software errors, API call performance, and page load times. The collected data does not include personally identifiable information (PII). It may, however, contain details such as device model, operating system, IP address, and general device information to assist in debugging and improving performance. This processing is necessary for the secure and reliable operation of the Application and is therefore carried out without requiring your explicit consent, on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 7. Disclosure of data

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose personal data to third parties only where:

- you have given your consent;
- disclosure is necessary to fulfil a contract with you (e.g. to backend/infrastructure service providers who process data on our behalf);
- we are subject to a legal obligation to disclose the data; or
- disclosure is necessary to protect our legitimate interests, such as investigating fraud or security incidents.

Any third-party service providers we use (such as our error-monitoring and backend infrastructure providers) act as data processors under a data processing agreement and are contractually bound to process data only as instructed by us.

## 8. Transfer of data to third countries

Some of our service providers (including our error-monitoring and backend infrastructure providers) may process data on servers located outside the European Union / European Economic Area ("third countries"). Where this is the case, we ensure an adequate level of data protection through appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or an applicable adequacy decision.

## 9. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy:

- Technical/error-monitoring data is retained in accordance with our service provider's standard retention settings, after which it is automatically deleted.
- Contact/feedback form data is retained only until your enquiry has been resolved, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

## 10. Your rights

- access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15);
- request rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
- request erasure of your data (Art. 17);
- request restriction of processing (Art. 18);
- receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller (data portability, Art. 20);
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21); and
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at <info@zttl.app>.
